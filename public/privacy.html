Data Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's how Masika Benevolent collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Overview

Masika Benevolent ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Data Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you interact with our services.

We are committed to:
Protecting your personal information, being transparent about our data practices, complying with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, and giving you control over your own data.

This policy applies to all members, prospective members, and visitors to our website and services. By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Contact us: if you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach our Data Protection Officer at dpo@masikabbs.com or +254 (0) 703 738 707.

Legal Framework

Masika Benevolent operates in compliance with:

  • Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 — the primary data protection law in Kenya
  • GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation) — for our international members
  • Kenya Constitution, Article 31 — the right to privacy
  • Kenya Consumer Protection Act, 2012 — consumer rights protection

What Data We Collect

We collect the following types of personal information to provide and improve our services:

Category Data collected Purpose
Personal identity Full name, ID/passport number, date of birth, gender Member identification and verification
Contact information Phone number, email address, physical address Communication and service delivery
Membership data Member number, plan type, registration date, payment history Membership management and billing
Family information Dependant names, relationships, dates of birth Family coverage and benefits
Payment information M-Pesa receipts, payment amounts, payment dates Payment processing and records
Technical data IP address, device information, browser type, cookies Website analytics and security

Sensitive data: we do not collect sensitive data such as medical records, biometric data, or racial/ethnic origin unless explicitly required and with your consent.

How We Collect Data

We collect data through:

  • Direct registration: information you provide during member registration
  • Member interactions: communications with our staff and customer service
  • Website use: automatic collection through cookies and analytics
  • M-Pesa payments: transaction records from payment processing
  • Third-party services: verification services where required

Children's data: we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we discover such data, we will delete it promptly.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide, improve, and personalize our services:

Service delivery

  • Process member registrations and renewals
  • Manage family coverage and dependants
  • Facilitate benefit claims and payments
  • Communicate important updates and notifications

Payment processing

  • Process M-Pesa payments and confirmations
  • Maintain accurate payment records
  • Send payment receipts and reminders

Improvement and analytics

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our services
  • Personalize member experience
  • Develop new products and services

Legal compliance

  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Prevent fraud and unauthorized activities
  • Protect the rights and safety of members

We do not: sell your personal data to third parties, share your data for marketing without consent, or use your data in ways not described in this policy.

Data Sharing

We may share your data with:

  • Service providers: payment processors, cloud storage, email services
  • Legal authorities: when required by law or to prevent harm
  • With your consent: when you explicitly authorize sharing

All third-party service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the purposes specified.

Your Data Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and GDPR, you have the following rights:

Right to access

Request a copy of your personal data

Right to rectification

Correct inaccurate or incomplete data

Right to deletion

Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")

Right to restrict

Limit how we process your data

Right to portability

Transfer your data to another provider

Right to object

Object to certain data processing

To exercise your rights, contact:
Data Protection Officer · dpo@masikabbs.com · +254 (0) 703 738 707

Consent Management

You can manage your consent preferences at any time:

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide services and fulfill legal obligations:

  • Active members: data retained for the duration of membership plus 5 years
  • Former members: data retained for 7 years for legal and tax purposes
  • Inactive members: data archived after 12 months of inactivity
  • Payment records: retained for 10 years (legal requirement)

You may request early deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.

Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your personal information:

Encryption

All data encrypted in transit (SSL/TLS) and at rest

Access control

Strict role-based access and authentication

Monitoring

Continuous security monitoring and threat detection

Backup

Regular secure backups and disaster recovery

Data breach protocol: in the event of a data breach, we will investigate and contain it immediately, notify affected individuals within 72 hours, report to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, and take corrective action to prevent future incidents.

Cookie Policy

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience:

  • Essential cookies: required for basic website functionality
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how users interact with our site
  • Preference cookies: remember your preferences and settings

You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect website functionality.
